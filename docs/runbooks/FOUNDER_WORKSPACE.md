# Founder workspace

Use the IELTS Lab mark as supplied, with its original proportions and details.
Start with the brand's crimson, charcoal and warm white. Let rounded surfaces,
quiet transitions and generous spacing carry the visual identity. Reserve
strong color for decisions, outcomes and errors. Avoid clutter, decorative
illustrations, emoji, startup slogans, glass effects and long explanatory copy.

Follow Apple Human Interface Guidelines for hierarchy, consistency, feedback,
accessibility and recovery. Use succinct labels and meaningful icons.
Use visuals, figures and touch-first controls where they shorten explanation.
Provide keyboard and text equivalents. Respect reduced motion. Keep layouts
usable on small phones and test long labels, empty states and failed saves.

The founder opens one workspace to review applicants and availability, manage
campaigns and posters, and reach payment and Speaking tools. Put the next
action in the first scan of each screen. Keep editing near the item, show
short save feedback and use Archive and Restore for removal. Keep printed
QR identifiers stable. Use a short, dismissible orientation in the workspace
and guide first use at the point of action.

The workspace and its marketing/application APIs require ADMIN and MFA.
Examiner and learner tools use their own permissions.
