# Separate short diagnostic — design for review

Status: design and defaults approved by owner; implementation authorized without
further routine approval gates. Not approved for release. Existing scaffold is
unfinished and has not been deployed. This design supersedes conflicting notes.

## Outcome and boundaries

Offer a practical IELTS Lab diagnostic taking 60 minutes of written work plus
a separately administered 7-minute in-person Speaking assessment. Authentic
question/task formats matter; practicality takes precedence over full-exam length.
This is not a full IELTS mock, official IELTS result, or certified score.

The existing mock test remains unchanged: routes, screens, content, timers,
entitlements, charging, grading, reports, Speaking bookings and saved attempts.
Diagnostic attempts must not consume existing full-mock purchases or device slots
through an accidental shared creation path. Do not modify the old mock runner,
manifest parser or grading contract to make the diagnostic fit.

Use a new diagnostic route family, services, profile and report. Reuse stable
utilities and existing domain tables only where doing so preserves these boundaries.
Use the current Next.js/Prisma/Supabase/QStash architecture, not a second host,
queue provider, authentication system or generalized assessment framework.

## Proposed format

| Stage | Duration | Content and delivery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preflight | Untimed | Explain format, verify audio, confirm readiness |
| Listening | 20 min | Complete harder Parts 3 and 4; 20 scoring slots |
| Reading | 20 min | One complete representative Academic passage; 13–14 slots |
| Writing | 20 min | One Task 2 prompt under explicitly shortened conditions |
| Speaking | 7 min separately | Part 1: 2 min; preparation: 1 min; Part 2: 90 sec; Part 3: 150 sec |

Retain original published source text. Explain shortened conditions outside source
instructions. Listening audio must be genuinely bounded to the selected parts,
human checked, hash-bound and fit inside 18 minutes, leaving two minutes total
preview/review allowance. If suitable licensed/reusable source content is absent,
the diagnostic is not ready; never fill the gap with invented official exercises.

## Speaking options and recommendation

1. **Record, then analyse (recommended):** examiner conducts the in-person test;
   consented audio is saved; analysis runs afterwards; examiner reviews and releases
   feedback. Lowest distraction and fewer live-network dependencies.
2. **Live copilot:** prompts/analysis arrive during the conversation. Adds latency,
   distraction and partial-evidence risks. Defer live AI commentary from version one.
3. **Human only:** no recording/AI. Keep this available when consent is declined,
   hardware fails or recording is unsuitable. Assessment still proceeds.

Version one can display a quiet examiner timer and standard prompts during the
interview, but AI feedback never drives the interview or interrupts the candidate.
No video needed. Candidate and examiner turns must be distinguishable; otherwise
ask for examiner correction or abstain from evidence-dependent scoring.
Audio may support pronunciation observations; a transcript alone must not.
AI output is advisory, timestamp-grounded, versioned and never silently released.
Short or poor-quality evidence produces an explicit limitation, not invented certainty.

## Candidate and examiner flow

Candidate: diagnostic introduction → access/identity → preflight → Listening →
Reading → Writing → submission receipt → Speaking status → approved report.
Booking/joining Speaking must not restart or mutate the written session.

Examiner: authorized roster → verify participant → confirm recording consent →
microphone check → conduct timed sections → save human notes → finalize upload →
analysis pending → inspect evidence and edit feedback → approve report.
Staff operations require server-side authorization and AAL2 for privileged actions.
Candidate APIs never return examiner-private notes or other participants' records.

## Timing, saving and recovery

- Server time is authoritative. Freeze section deadlines when the session starts;
  tab changes, refresh, device clock changes and sleep cannot extend them.
- Server rejects writes to expired sections, even if the client countdown is stale.
  UI locks/submits at its next execution opportunity. Browsers cannot promise an
  exact-millisecond visible cutoff while suspended; server enforcement can.
- Save responses with monotonically ordered revisions and acknowledgements.
  Never show 'saved' before acknowledgement; do not let stale retries overwrite
  newer responses. Preserve unsent local work where safe, with explicit status.
- Only one active writer per session. Concurrent tabs/retries cannot create two
  sessions or consume two access grants. Ownership is checked on every operation.
- No answer keys, rubric secrets or privileged access decisions reach the browser.
- Network/audio failure is recorded and recoverable. No silent clock reset, replay
  or extension; staff can authorize a documented retry/accommodation.
- Keep a server-authoritative terminal state and idempotent submission. Grading
  failure leaves recoverable pending work rather than an apparently empty report.

## Report contract

Listening and Reading: raw marks, denominators, answered counts, question-type
strengths and limitations. Do not linearly convert 20/13 questions into a 40-item band.
Writing: Task 2-only criterion feedback and useful next steps, acknowledging the
shorter time; do not apply the full Task 1/Task 2 weighted formula.
Speaking: examiner-approved observations supported by evidence, with any AI use
and limitations clear. No automated pass/fail, immigration eligibility or official
overall band. Prioritize at most three actionable improvement points.

## Visual and accessibility contract

IELTS Lab SVG logo; existing crimson, charcoal and white; restrained rounded
controls, generous whitespace, one primary action and one task at a time.
Glass is limited to small navigation/control surfaces. Text/passage/essay surfaces
remain opaque and readable. No excessive blur, floating decorations or dense cards.
Localized interface in English, French and Arabic; source English test content stays
English. Arabic chrome must not reverse English questions, numbers or audio controls.
Keyboard alternatives to dragging, touch-friendly targets, screen-reader names,
visible focus, zoom/reflow, safe areas, reduced motion/transparency and high contrast.

## Cost-controlled execution after plan approval

Use GPT-6.1 Sol for scoped implementation and regression testing. Give it the
approved spec, exact task files/interfaces, failure criteria and commands rather
than the entire conversation. One bounded task at a time, no overlapping writes.
The primary agent retains architecture decisions, access/privacy boundaries,
review of timer/ownership/data-loss risks and the final release decision.
Do not delegate open-ended production/provider changes, real payments or legal
policy invention. Preserve unrelated work. No live launch before review gates pass.

Proposed task sequence:
1. Isolated diagnostic manifest/schema tests; remove diagnostic coupling from the
   ordinary manifest parser without discarding the saved work.
2. Additive persistence and access policy; server deadlines/autosave/resume;
   real database concurrency and ownership tests.
3. Separate localized candidate UI, raw-mark/Task2-only reporting and accessibility.
4. Examiner in-person workflow, explicit consent and private resumable recording.
5. Post-session AI jobs, evidence validation and human report approval.
6. Content readiness/provisioning using real approved material; browser/Playwright
   release matrix and old-mock regression comparison.

## Access decision — confirmed by owner

Free for staff-invited participants only. No public self-enrolment, checkout,
payment dependency or use of a full-mock entitlement. Staff controls invitations
and separate Speaking arrangements. Account identity and invitation ownership
are checked server-side; possession of a session UUID never grants access.

Proposed invitation defaults: account-bound invitation, single diagnostic attempt,
seven days to start; resuming the same attempt is not another redemption. Staff
can revoke an unused invitation or authorize an audited replacement for a failed
session. Do not silently grant unlimited retries or alter the old device policy.

## Privacy defaults — approved by owner

Recording/AI is optional with explicit consent; audio and transcript
are automatically deleted after 30 days from capture. Approved feedback remains
available for 90 days from release, then is deleted from diagnostic report storage.
These are approved product retention periods, not a claim of legal sufficiency;
provider retention and backup deletion limits must also be verified and disclosed.

No recording or AI processing of minors until a suitable guardian-consent process
has been established. Human-only Speaking remains possible without recording.
Do not silently repurpose the existing mock-test policy. Recording stays off until
consent, applicable policy, provider behavior and deletion are configured and tested.
